# Verena Teresa Uyka

> Research-oriented AI builder exploring how intelligent systems can remain reliable when their models, observations, or constraints are imperfect.

I build small, reproducible research-engineering projects around uncertainty,
evaluation, robustness, and evidence-grounded AI.

## Research focus

My current portfolio is organised around one question:

> **How can AI systems remain reliable when their models, observations, or constraints are imperfect?**

I am particularly interested in calibrated uncertainty, selective prediction,
reproducible evaluation, and systems that make their limitations inspectable
rather than hiding them.

## Working principles

- Start with a precise question and an explicit comparison.
- Treat evaluation as part of the system, not an afterthought.
- Prefer reproducible experiments over impressive-looking one-off outputs.
- Measure failure modes alongside aggregate success metrics.
- Document limitations and non-claims explicitly.
